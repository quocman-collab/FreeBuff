Introduction
Overview
The Home Share - Boarding House Management System is a comprehensive mobile platform designed to modernize and streamline boarding house management, room rentals, and shared accommodation. The application provides an integrated digital environment serving three key user groups:
Tenants (Renters): Users who can browse verified property listings, submit rental requests for rooms or entire houses, manage their stay, and communicate directly with property owners via real-time messaging.
Landlords (Property Owners): Users equipped with tools to list rental properties, manage house and room inventories, track tenant records, monitor rental income and utility payments, and interact with prospective/current tenants.
Administrators (System Admins): Supervisors responsible for platform content moderation, publishing system-wide announcements, and overseeing transaction commission management.
Scope
The system is developed as a practical rental accommodation management platform for managing rental properties, rooms, tenants, and related rental activities.
Platform: Cross-platform mobile application developed using Flutter.
Supported platforms: Android.
Supported Android version: Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above.
User roles: Admin, Landlord, and Tenant.
Test device: Android Emulator / Physical Android Device.
Supported screen orientation: Portrait mode.

Feature/Component #1: Admin Dashboard & Financial Report Screen
User Interfaces
Financial & Operational KPI Tracking: Provides admins with a quick overview of system-wide metrics, including total cash flow, net platform revenue (service/commission fees), completed transactions, and total registered users.
Revenue Visualization (Analytics & Charts): Time-series charts (Week / Month / Year) help analyze revenue growth trends and identify peak performance periods.
Actionable Alerts & Urgent Tasks: Displays urgent notifications requiring immediate action, such as approving deposit disbursements to landlords.
System Navigation: Provides quick shortcuts to other management modules, including Listings, Order Management, and Transaction History.
